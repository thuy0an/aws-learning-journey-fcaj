# General Feedback on the Internship at Bootcamp First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)

## 1. Learning and Working Environment

During **Bootcamp First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)**, I found the learning environment professional and friendly. Although the program follows a Bootcamp model rather than a traditional company model, its learning and project process is clear and helps learners become familiar with real working practices.

I was especially impressed by the encouragement to learn actively and share knowledge. When I faced project challenges, I received support from mentors and team members. This helped me solve problems and motivated me to continue learning.

## 2. Support from Mentors and the Admin Team

Mentors provided detailed guidance. When I had difficulties with AWS services or system architecture, they explained the causes and guided my thinking instead of only giving ready-made answers.

They also shared real experience about developing and operating cloud systems, which gave me a clearer view of the Cloud Engineer role and the skills I still need to improve. The Admin Team supported learning materials, schedules, events, and other program-related questions quickly, allowing me to focus on learning and project work.

## 3. Fit with My Field of Study

The FCAJ internship content fits well with my Information Technology major. While school mainly provided foundation knowledge in programming, networking, and databases, the Bootcamp gave me direct experience with technologies commonly used in enterprises.

I learned and practiced AWS services such as EC2, S3, IAM, VPC, RDS, ECS, ECR, Application Load Balancer, CloudWatch, AWS Budgets, and CloudFront. I also learned about the **AWS Well-Architected Framework**, multi-tier architecture, CI/CD, and cloud optimization principles.

## 4. Learning and Skill Development Opportunities

Through **NeonFoodMap – Vinh Khanh Food Street Automated Audio Guide**, I learned task planning, task allocation, teamwork, and progress management. When deployment issues happened, I practiced analyzing causes, searching technical documentation, and fixing problems step by step.

My English technical reading, idea presentation, AWS architecture-diagram design, and project-reporting skills also improved significantly.

## 5. Culture and Team Spirit

The FCAJ community has a strong culture of knowledge sharing and mutual support. Each member is responsible for their own work while remaining willing to help others. Mentors also create opportunities for learners to present ideas, discuss, and contribute feedback.

Even though the Bootcamp period was not long, I felt the community's teamwork and professionalism. This is one of the main reasons I have a positive impression of the program.

## Personal Reflection After the Internship

FCAJ was a meaningful experience for my learning and career direction. I was pleased to practice directly on AWS, build a project with my team, and apply knowledge to a real system.

Besides technical knowledge, I improved skills in teamwork, progress management, technical research, idea presentation, and problem solving. These experiences make me more confident about entering a professional working environment after graduation.

In my opinion, the program has a clear learning path and dedicated mentors. In the future, it could include more sharing sessions from Cloud Engineers or Solution Architects working in enterprises, as well as more projects with larger and more realistic scenarios.

I would recommend FCAJ to students interested in Cloud Computing, DevOps, or AI because it offers practical content and strong support from the community. I hope to join advanced FCAJ programs on AWS Solution Architecture, DevOps, or AI on AWS in the future.

Finally, I sincerely thank the mentors, Admin Team, and organizers of Bootcamp First Cloud AI Journey for their guidance and support. This experience helped me grow in technical knowledge, work skills, and problem-solving thinking.
